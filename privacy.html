Privacy Policy

Chicken Tender, Inc  •  Effective November 4, 2026  •  Applies to app version 2.3 and newer

No account, ever

No email address, no password, no login. What we collect is held against a random identifier for your install, not your name.

Location stays purposeful

Your location is used to find restaurants near you and is never published to other players.

No ads, no ad tracking

No advertising in the app, no advertising identifier, and nothing shared with ad networks.

Chicken Tender, Inc (“Chicken Tender”, “we”, “us”) makes the Chicken Tender™ app (the “app”), a free app that helps groups decide where to eat. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Using the app is also governed by our Terms of Use.

The app never asks for your name, email address or phone number. What we collect is held against a random identifier for your install, along with information such as your location, your activity in the app, your favorites and any visits you record at a restaurant. We don’t try to work out who you are from this information.

01What we collect

02Why we collect it

The purposes named in the table above mean the following:

  • App functionality — making the app work: loading nearby restaurants, keeping a game in sync between phones, remembering your filters, and showing your results.
  • Analytics — understanding how the app is used in aggregate so we can fix problems and improve it.
  • Product personalization — tailoring what you see, such as which restaurants surface first and which of them are worth showing again.
  • Our own advertising and marketing — measuring whether our own campaigns work and reaching people who might like the app. This never involves an advertising identifier or an ad network; see section 5.
  • Messages from restaurants — delivering offers and Chicken Tender Rewards updates from restaurants you favorite; see section 7 for how to turn them off.

03What we don’t collect

  • No account credentials. The app never asks for an email address, a password, or a login, and we have no user accounts to attach data to.
  • No contacts or microphone access. The app does not request either.
  • No account profile. The display name and photo you can add stay on your device. They are shared over Bluetooth with the players in your game and are never sent to us. Your favorites, any note or menu items you attach to them, codes for offers you claim, and your Chicken Tender Rewards activity are things we do keep for you on our servers — see section 1. They are held against the random identifier for your install, not against a name or an email address.
  • No crash logs or diagnostics. The app does not collect crash reports, performance measurements, or other technical diagnostics from your device.
  • No payment information. The app is free and has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. Claiming an offer or earning a reward involves no payment through the app, and we never learn what you ordered or spent.
  • No location check for Chicken Tender Rewards. A visit is recorded when staff enter a code. The app does not use your location to detect or confirm a visit, although we do keep the record of each visit described in section 1.
  • No advertising identifier. The app does not read or use the advertising ID your operating system provides — IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android.
  • No advertising. Chicken Tender displays no ads, and shares nothing with advertising networks or third-party advertisers.

04Bluetooth and nearby players

Chicken Tender uses Bluetooth to discover other phones running the app nearby, so a group can play together without sharing links or creating accounts. This is a direct, device-to-device connection between the phones in your group.

  • If you add a display name and photo, they are broadcast over Bluetooth to the other players in your game so everyone can tell whose vote is whose. They are shared only while you are playing, and they never reach our servers.
  • Your swipes are exchanged with the other players in your game so the app can work out what everyone agreed on.
  • A name and photo are optional. You can play without either.
  • Bluetooth is used to find players nearby. It is not used to track your movements or to build a location history.

05Analytics and advertising

Chicken Tender shows no advertising, does not use the advertising identifier your operating system provides, and does not work with third-party advertisers or ad networks. No advertising data is collected, and nothing is shared with anyone for the purpose of advertising to you.

We do look at how the app is used in aggregate — which features people reach, which restaurants get swiped on — so we can fix problems and improve it. That analysis is our own rather than a third-party analytics service’s, and it does not use your name or email address, because we never ask for them.

We also advertise Chicken Tender itself in the ordinary way, on other platforms. That is our own marketing and it does not involve tracking you: we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not hand your data to an ad network to target you.

A plain statement about “selling” and “sharing”: we do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or targeted advertising as those terms are defined in U.S. state privacy laws.

06How we share information

We use no third-party analytics vendors and no advertising vendors. We do use the service providers named below to run the app. Otherwise we share information only in the circumstances that follow.

  • Other players in your game — over Bluetooth, as described in section 4.
  • Apple Maps, when you search from outside the United States — our own restaurant catalog covers the United States. If you are traveling abroad and it has nothing near you, the app can search Apple Maps instead so you still get a deck. The coordinates you are searching from are sent from our servers to Apple in order to run that search, and the results come back to you as cards. The identifier for your install is not sent to Apple, and Apple’s privacy policy governs what Apple does with the search.
  • Service providers — Amazon Web Services hosts our servers and databases. Apple and Google deliver notifications to your device, so they handle the text of each message, including personalized Chicken Tender Rewards progress. They also help us confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app. On Android, maps in the app are provided by Google Maps. Each company’s own privacy policy governs what it does with information it handles for its own purposes.
  • The restaurant whose offer you claimed — when you show a code, the restaurant’s staff can look it up in Chicken Tender for Merchants and see the offer and terms it was issued for, when it was claimed and when it expires, and whether it has been used. They do not see the identifier for your install, your display name or photo, or your location. Restaurants also see how many of their offers were claimed and used, as totals.
  • The restaurant where you use Chicken Tender Rewards — staff who enter your code see your progress and any reward. Owners see totals for the program, such as how many people joined, how many visits were recorded and how many rewards were earned and used. To help them review staff activity, owners may also see that a customer visited on many consecutive days, under a label unique to that restaurant, along with the staff device or login that recorded those visits. They do not see the identifier for your install, your display name or photo, or your location.
  • Restaurants you favorite — a restaurant sees how many people favorited it and how many people its messages reached, but not who they are. At a restaurant with few favorites, those totals can be small numbers.
  • Restaurants and merchants — aggregated swipe trends may be provided to restaurants through Chicken Tender for Merchants, so they can understand how diners in an area are choosing. These trends do not include your name or the identifier for your install, and no individual’s swipes are ever shown to a restaurant.
  • Legal requirements — if we are required to disclose information by law, such as in response to a valid subpoena or court order, or to protect our rights, safety, or property.
  • Business transfers — if Chicken Tender, Inc is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.
  • With your consent — where you have explicitly asked us to.

Links out of the app

Restaurant listings, hours, photos and popular times are served from our own systems. Menus, ordering and reservation links, directions and phone numbers point to services run by the restaurant or by another company — the restaurant’s own site, your maps app, your phone dialer. Once you follow one of those you have left Chicken Tender, and that service’s privacy policy governs what happens there. Opening the details of a restaurant found through Apple Maps, rather than in our own catalog, opens that place’s Apple Maps page in the in-app browser in the same way.

Pages in your browser

If you open a Chicken Tender Rewards QR code or a shared restaurant link in a browser instead of the app, or visit this website, the page loads fonts from Google, so your browser’s IP address reaches Google. Google’s privacy policy governs that.

07Your choices and controls

  • Location. Grant, downgrade, or revoke location access at any time in your device settings. Without it, you can still pin a neighborhood manually and play.
  • Tracking. There is nothing to opt out of. The app does not ask to track you, does not use your advertising identifier, and does not share data with advertisers.
  • Bluetooth. Revoke Bluetooth access in device settings. You can still play solo.
  • Notifications. You can turn off all notifications from the app at any time in device settings.
  • Messages from restaurants. Restaurants you favorite may send you offers and Chicken Tender Rewards updates as notifications, and we limit how many you can receive. To turn these off without affecting notices from Chicken Tender itself, use Messages from Restaurants: on iOS, in the Settings app under Chicken Tender; on Android, in your profile in the app. Removing a favorite also stops that restaurant’s messages.
  • Display name and photo. Both are optional and can be changed or removed in the app at any time. Neither is stored on our servers.
  • Your favorites. Remove a favorite in the app at any time and its note and menu items go with it. Removing them this way is the quickest way to have them deleted from our servers.
  • Offers you claim. Claiming an offer is always your choice. You can give back a code you haven’t used at any time in the app.
  • Chicken Tender Rewards. Joining is always your choice, and it adds the restaurant to your favorites. Removing the favorite stops new visits from counting there, but it does not delete your saved visits or rewards. To have those deleted, email us.
  • Deleting the app. Deleting the app removes the app’s own storage on your device, including your display name and photo. It does not delete your favorites, the codes you have claimed or your Chicken Tender Rewards activity, which are held on our servers rather than on your phone, and it does not remove the identifier for your install — that is kept deliberately, as described in section 1, so that reinstalling or moving to a new phone brings your own favorites back rather than starting you over. If you want your favorites gone, remove them in the app before you delete it, or email us. To have your Chicken Tender Rewards activity deleted, email us.
  • Requests to us. Email info@chickentenderapp.com to ask about, opt out of, or request deletion of data associated with your install. Because the app has no accounts, please include your device platform and the approximate dates you used the app so we can locate the relevant records.

08Data retention

Usage and swipe data is retained for as long as it is useful for the purposes described above, and aggregated trend data may be retained indefinitely in a form that does not identify any device or person.

Your favorites — including any note and menu items you added — are kept until you remove them, in the app or by asking us. They are held against the identifier for your install, and because that identifier is meant to outlive the install, deleting the app does not delete them. On Android, restoring the identifier depends on your device backup being switched on; if it is not, a reinstall starts you with a new list, and the old one stays with us until you ask us to delete it.

A code you claim stays in the app until you use it, give it back, or it expires. A code you have used stays visible in the app for 30 days so you can check whether you already used an offer. Our record that an offer was claimed, and whether it was used, is kept as part of the restaurant’s reporting on its offers, in the same way as the usage data above.

Your Chicken Tender Rewards progress, recorded visits and rewards are kept as part of the restaurant’s program and reporting, in the same way as the usage data above, until you ask us to delete them or they are removed with the restaurant’s listing. A reward stays available in the app until you use it or it expires. Removing a favorite or deleting the app does not delete this activity.

Our record that a restaurant’s message was sent to your install is kept for a limited time so we can enforce limits on how many you receive, and is then deleted.

Data held only on your device, including your display name and photo, is removed when you delete the app.

09Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect, including encryption in transit. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 16 has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, to request its deletion or correction, to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and of targeted advertising, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights. We do not sell or share personal information and we do not run targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of on that front. To exercise any of your other rights, use the device-level controls in section 7 or email us at info@chickentenderapp.com.

Chicken Tender is offered in the United States, and this policy is written for United States users. The only official distribution channels for the app are the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store, and this policy applies only to versions of the app obtained through those channels.
