This page sets out who owns the Chicken Tender™ brand, the terms that govern its use, and how to ask permission. It exists so that anyone who wants to use the mark — or who is unsure whether they may — has a clear answer and somewhere to write to.

01What this covers

  • The flame logo. The flame-and-drumstick mark used as the app icon and throughout this site, in every colour, size and variation.
  • The name. “Chicken Tender” as the name of the app and the company.
  • The wordmark. The “ChickenTender” lockup as it is set on this site and in the app.
  • The tagline. “End the ‘where do we eat?’ debate”.

02Who owns it

03Terms of use

All rights are reserved. Apart from the press and editorial use described in section 04, publishing these terms is not permission to use the brand; it tells you what the position is and who to ask. Unless you have written permission from us, please do not:

  • Use the logo, the name, or the wordmark in a way that suggests we endorse, sponsor, or are affiliated with you, your product, or your business.
  • Modify the logo — recolour it, redraw it, rotate it, add to it, crop it, or pull it apart.
  • Fold the logo or the name into a mark of your own, including app icons, favicons, logos, avatars, and merchandise.
  • Use the brand as the name of, or on, a product or service that is not ours.
  • Register the name, the logo, or anything confusingly similar as a trademark, domain, or account handle.

Nothing here is meant to limit the things the law allows regardless of permission, such as referring to Chicken Tender by name to report on it, review it, or compare it.

04Press and editorial use

If you are writing about Chicken Tender — a news story, a review, a blog post, a roundup — you may use the flame logo and the wordmark to illustrate it without asking us first. You do not need written permission for that, and we would rather you used the real thing than something pulled off a screenshot.

This permission comes with three conditions:

  • Use it as it is. No recolouring, redrawing, cropping, rotating, or adding to it.
  • Use it to refer to us. Not in a way that suggests we endorse, sponsor, or are affiliated with your publication, product, or service.
  • Editorial only. This does not extend to advertising, merchandise, packaging, or anything sold, and it does not let you use the mark as an identifier of your own.

Ask for the press kit

We keep a press kit — the logo in the formats you actually want, app screenshots, and boilerplate about the company. Email info@chickentenderapp.com and we will send it over. If you are working to a deadline, say so in the subject line.

05Asking permission

We would rather say yes to something specific than guess at what you meant, so write to info@chickentenderapp.com and tell us:

  • Who you are, and the company you are writing on behalf of, if any.
  • Which of the assets above you want to use.
  • Where it would appear, and for how long.
  • Whether the context is commercial.

Permission, when we give it, is in writing and covers only what it describes.

06Reporting misuse

If you have seen the Chicken Tender name or logo used somewhere it shouldn’t be, we would like to know. Email info@chickentenderapp.com with a link or a screenshot.
