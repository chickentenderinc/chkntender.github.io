// questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Chicken Tender?

Chicken Tender is a free mobile app that ends the “I don’t know, where do you want to go?” loop. Everyone pulls out their phone, everyone swipes on nearby restaurants, and the first place you all say yes to is dinner.

Do I need an account?

No. Chicken Tender doesn’t need an email address, a password, or a login — just open it and start swiping. Adding a display name and photo is optional and only helps friends know whose vote is whose. Both stay on your device and are shared over Bluetooth with the players in your game, only while you play — they are never sent to us.

How does the group matching work?

Everyone just opens the app. Chicken Tender uses Bluetooth to automatically discover nearby players — no invites, no links to share, zero setup. Everyone swipes the same set of nearby restaurants, and the moment everyone likes the same place you’ve got a match. End the game to reveal it, along with everything else the group liked.

What’s new in 2.0?

Every screen has been redesigned. Open Now is on by default so you never match on a closed restaurant. Cards now carry hours, a description, highlights and popular times, and pulling up gives you the menu, ordering and reservation links, a map, and one-tap directions and calling. You can filter by cuisine, sort by distance, and pin a different neighborhood. Bluetooth pairing is dramatically more reliable. Note: 2.0 changes how phones talk to each other, so everyone in your group needs to update to play together.

Is Chicken Tender free?

Yes — free to download and free to use, with no ads in the app, no in-app purchases and no subscription.

Does it work for just two people, or even solo?

Absolutely. It works great for couples on date night, families picking dinner, and large groups of friends. No friends around? Swipe solo and let Chicken Tender pick for you — perfect when you’re travelling or exploring a new neighborhood.

What devices are supported?

Chicken Tender works on iOS and Android smartphones and tablets. The two versions look and work the same, so everyone can play together regardless of what they carry.

What data does the app collect?

Chicken Tender has no accounts, so nothing it collects is tied to your identity. It uses your location to find restaurants near you and records how the app is used so we can improve it. No crash logs, no ads, no advertising identifier, and nothing shared with advertisers. Our Privacy Policy lists every category.
